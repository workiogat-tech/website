How to edit the homepage hero
  1. Change Welcome to SJS and the subtitle under Main / Sub Headline below. Pick font and font color next to each.
  2. Edit Explore Events and Schedule a Visit under Hero Buttons (label, link, background color, text color).
  3. Click Save Changes, then refresh the homepage. On phones the Explore Events text stays white on the school maroon so it stays readable.
