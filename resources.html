Resources & Information

Access important documents, policies, and resources to support your child's education.

Parent Handbook

The Parent Handbook provides clear guidelines on school policies, academic expectations, student behaviour, and communication between the school and parents. It helps parents understand school routines, responsibilities and support available for their children's education.

Academic Support

Parents are expected to support their children's learning by ensuring regular attendance, completing homework, and providing learning materials. They should communicate with teachers, attend meetings, and monitor academic progress. Parental support helps improve discipline, motivation, and overall student achievement.

Health & Safety

The school is committed to providing a safe and healthy learning environment for all learners and staff. Basic health services, hygiene practices, and safety rules are in place to prevent accidents and illness. Parents are encouraged to cooperate with the school to ensure children follow health and safety guidelines.

Transportation

Bus schedules and transportation policies. The school provides safe and reliable transportation services for learners using the school bus. All buses operate on designated routes and follow strict safety rules to protect learners. Parents are advised to ensure timely drop-off and pickup at assigned bus points.
