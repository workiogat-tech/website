Admission

🎒 Nursery

Our nursery program provides a nurturing and stimulating environment for young learners. Children are introduced to foundational concepts through play-based learning, developing social skills, creativity, and early literacy. To receive an admission letter, parents must pay an admission fee of 40,000 Ugandan Shillings at the school.

📚 Primary

Our primary education program offers a comprehensive curriculum that builds strong academic foundations. Students develop critical thinking, problem-solving skills, and a love for learning across all subjects. To receive an admission letter, parents must pay an admission fee of 40,000 Ugandan Shillings at the school.
